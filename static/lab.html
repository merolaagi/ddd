Protein structure · AlphaFold view

The AlphaFold view of a target: its precomputed AlphaFold DB structure, coloured by per-residue confidence (pLDDT), next to the predicted aligned error (PAE) map — the same two panels the AlphaFold viewer shows. Fetches a known protein's existing prediction (not a de-novo fold of a new sequence).

Type a target and fetch its AlphaFold structure + PAE.

Generative design · our Chemistry42

De-novo molecule design: a graph genetic algorithm evolves novel, drug-like candidates for a target, seeded from its ChEMBL actives (or your own SMILES) and scored by QED + synthesizability + Ro5 + similarity. Click any candidate to generate its real 3D conformer (ETKDG + MMFF). Reward-guided search over public chemistry — not validated activity.

Type a target gene (or paste seed SMILES) and Design. ~15–40s for ChEMBL seeds, then the GA runs locally.
Click a candidate to build its 3D structure.

Virtual Cell · live

A cell built from the graph — a disease’s target proteins, each placed in its real subcellular compartment (PrimeKG cellular-component annotations), with the protein–protein links among them. Not a simulation: a data-driven map that moves.

Enter a disease and press Build cell. Proteins sit in their real compartment; size = how many drugs already touch them.
